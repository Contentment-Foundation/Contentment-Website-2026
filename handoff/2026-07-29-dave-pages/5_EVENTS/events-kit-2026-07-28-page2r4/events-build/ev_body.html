Why we gather

Our gatherings are the practice itself: the Four Pillars, experienced together.

Educators arrive depleted and leave reconnected. Members arrive curious and leave committed. Strangers leave as a community. We've watched it happen on four continents, and it never stops feeling like the whole point.

“We're with you” is the promise we make to every teacher. Our gatherings are where everyone gets to feel it.
