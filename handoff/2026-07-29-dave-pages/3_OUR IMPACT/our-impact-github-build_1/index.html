Bhutan

ELC High SchoolThimphu, Bhutan · video story

It started with a single school in Thimphu in 2019. Not a government mandate, not a national rollout, just one circle of educators willing to sit together and ask: what does it feel like to be well?

That question changed everything. ELC (Educating for Lifelong Citizenship) High School became a lighthouse school, a place where wellbeing isn’t delivered at certain hours but lived throughout the culture. From there the work spread, from one school to 50 across a valley, to annual national retreats for roughly 200 counselors, and into Bhutan’s National School Health and Wellbeing Strategy.

East Africa

Nyangʻori Boys High SchoolVihiga County, Kenya · video story

Many African educators carry extraordinary weight: overcrowded classrooms, student needs shaped by poverty and broken homes, and constant pressure at the front line of support.

Many were giving everything and running on empty, some close to leaving the profession. The Four Pillars of Wellbeing offered something different, an invitation to turn inward first and become, as one educator put it, a self-scientist. Across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and the DRC, teachers who found calm in themselves brought that calm to their classrooms. What began in Kenya in 2023 has taken root across the region, not by replication, but through local ownership. It turns out wellbeing is contagious.

Hawaiʻi

Deepening Educator WellbeingLevel 2 Educator Training, Hawaiʻi · video story

Hawaiʻi doesn’t need another wellness program. What educators across 10 schools on Oʻahu, Kauaʻi, Maui, and Hawaiʻi Island needed was a partner willing to slow down, listen, and earn the right to be present.

As Jennifer Gonsalves, the Foundation’s Hawaiʻi Program Lead, put it: trust is not given, it is earned, through consistency, humility, and respect for pilina (relationship). Here, change looks like a skeptical teacher becoming a champion, or a kumu (teacher) arriving on autopilot and leaving reconnected to her naʻau (center). This is what happens when wellbeing is offered as relationship, not program. He alo a he alo (face to face), until trust becomes the ground everything else grows from.

North America

Corbett Preparatory SchoolTampa, FL · the first pilot school · video story

In 2016, Corbett Preparatory School in Tampa became the first school in the world to say yes. Then Covid hit, and a digital platform carried the work to New York City schools through one of the hardest seasons educators had faced.

From there it traveled person to person, to Siskiyou County in California, to Lakeland in Florida, and beyond. A Corbett educator carried it with him to The Country School in California, as something he had lived, not a program in a binder. The Four Pillars of Wellbeing spread because educators who felt something real wanted others to feel it too.
